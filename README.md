# 🌌 Balatro: Reality Warp (v1.0.0)

A massive overhaul and content expansion for **Balatro**, featuring reality-bending mechanics, cosmic Outsiders, dark market economies, pantheon boss showdowns, and high-synergy Jokers.

---

## 📦 Requirements & Installation
1. Install **[Steamodded](https://github.com/Steamodded/smods)** (`v1.0.0+`) and **[Lovely](https://github.com/ethangreen-dev/lovely-injector)** (`v0.7.1+`).
2. Place `RealityWarp` (or download the release) into your Balatro `Mods` folder:
   - **Windows**: `%AppData%/Balatro/Mods/`
3. *(Optional)* **[CardSleeves](https://github.com/)** and **[JokerDisplay](https://github.com/nhandev/JokerDisplay)** for custom deck sleeves and real-time calculation overlays.

## 🔮 Core Systems

### 🌌 Outsiders & Warp Portal
* **Outsiders**: Unique, untyped cosmic Jokers with game-warping abilities that exist outside normal shop pools.
* **Warp Portal**: A dedicated Spectral consumable that tears open the fabric of reality to manifest a random Outsider directly into your lineup.

### 💰 Kyra's Black Market & Dark Money (€)
* Earn **Dark Money (€)** by defeating blinds (+€1 Small, +€2 Big, +€3 Boss, +€5 Showdown).
* Spend **€** at Kyra’s black market counter on forbidden Potions, rare Spectrals, stolen Jokers, and Legendaries.
* Features a dedicated HUD currency counter and €1 market rerolls.

### 🏛️ Battle of Gods (Post-Game Mode)
* High-stakes post-Ante 8 challenge mode (toggled in Mod Settings; requires **Amulet**).
* All blinds replaced with Boss Blinds; Ante finales pit you against Supreme Showdown Gods (**Chronos, Ares, Athena, Hades, Zeus**).
* Includes **12 Fused Boss Blinds (Ante 12+)**, **Boss Possession Stickers**, and **26 Nursery Familiar Companions** that level up as you play.

### 🧪 Potion Pouch & Cauldron Synthesis
* Dedicated HUD button (`[ 🧪 POUCH ]`) storing consumable alchemical brews without occupying regular consumable slots.
* Fuse consumables and Jokers inside the Alchemical Cauldron to summon higher-rarity Amalgams.

### 🎟️ Reworked Vouchers
* **Ambrosia**: Earn guaranteed **+$20** for each Blind defeated.
* **Nectar**: Permanently grants **-5% Chip Requirement**, **+1 Hand Size**, **+1 Voucher in Shop**, **+1 Shop Space**, and **+1 Hand** per round.

## 🎴 Included Decks
* **Colosseum**: Battle of Gods mode scaled to 24 Antes with starter vouchers and early tempo Jokers.
* **Dark Merchant**: Starts with +€10 Dark Money, bonus Boss € payouts, and guaranteed Black Market access.
* **Caveman**: Low rank deck where non-target cards become Stone cards.
* **Strategist**: Condensed 24-card deck (9 through Ace) starting with Magic Trick.
* **Overseer**: Generates Spectral cards each round; double tag triggers.
* **Friendly**: Starts with 2 random Negative Eternal Jokers.
* **Alchemist**: Starts with active Recurring Distillation and potion rate boosts.
